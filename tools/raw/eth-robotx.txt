With the RobotX PhD Fellowship 2027, you apply once and are considered by several RobotX research groups across ETH Zurich. You choose up to three professors you would like to work with, and they review your application. Application deadline: 15 December 2026. Job description The research groups of RobotX cover a broad spectrum of robotics, including but not limited to: Soft and Musculoskeletal Robotics: bioinspired robots built from compliant materials, artificial muscles, and tendons, for safe and adaptive interaction with an unstructured world. Robotic Locomotion and Manipulation: legged robots, robot arms, and hands that move and act reliably in complex terrain and contact-rich tasks. Biohybrid Robots: robots built from living cells, engineered tissue, and synthetic materials, actuated by muscle and powered by chemical energy. Perception and Computer Vision: 3D reconstruction, object recognition, and scene understanding that give robots reliable perception of their surroundings. Human-Robot Interaction: sensory-motor interaction between humans and machines for assistive, rehabilitative, and cooperative tasks. Computational Design and Fabrication: simulation, optimization, and learned surrogate models to design and fabricate efficient, bioinspired robots. Reinforcement Learning and Machine Learning: data-driven methods that let robots learn new skills and adapt to new tasks and environments. Humanoid Robotics and Tactile Sensing for Manipulation: humanoid loco-manipulation, whole-body control, and tactile sensing that let robots reason across touch and vision to handle objects dexterously. Motion Planning and Control: algorithms for navigation, control, and decision-making in dynamic environments. Safety-Critical Robotic Systems: robots that perform reliably under uncertainty, in particular when they work alongside humans. You define your doctoral project together with your supervisor during your first months. Profile A Master's degree in mechanical or electrical engineering, computer science, robotics, bioengineering, materials science, or a closely related field, completed by August 2027 An excellent academic record in both your Bachelor's and Master's studies Demonstrated experience in one or more of the research areas above, ideally a project you carried from idea to a working result on real hardware Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, and the drive to do independent research Strong programming skills; experience with robotics software frameworks is a plus Excellent written and spoken English Workplace Workplace We offer A fully funded doctoral position at ETH Zurich, employed at the ETH doctoral salary rate with full social benefits Supervision by a RobotX professor and access to the RobotX network of robotics groups across ETH Zurich Start date by agreement in 2027 Your career with impact: Become part of ETH Zurich, which not only supports your professional development, but also actively contributes to positive change in society. We are actively committed to a sustainable and climate-neutral university . We look forward to receiving your online application as one merged PDF file, ideally under 10 MB, with the documents in this order: Cover letter: your research motivation, what you have built so far, and which RobotX research groups you want to work with and why CV, including any publications or preprints Transcripts of your Bachelor's and Master's degrees in English, with the local grading scale Names and contact details of three references, each with an email address and a direct phone number One publication, thesis, or project report that shows your research and writing Name the PDF with the application date (YYMMDD) and your last and first name.

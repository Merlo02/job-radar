Other Staff Categories Employees on fixed-term contracts (CDD), doctoral assistants, postdocs, and student-assistants are subject to remuneration conditions that are independent of the ETH salary system. Administrative or Technical Staff on Fixed-Term Contracts The salary of individuals hired on fixed-term contracts (CDD) in administrative or technical positions is calculated based on the salary scale for permanent positions (CDI), taking into account age and relevant experience. Persons on fixed-term contracts employed in scientific positions are subject to different remuneration conditions. Although these employees are not automatically subject to EPFL’s salary system reserved for permanent staff, they may benefit from a standard annual increase according to their salary level, subject to available budgetary resources. Annual Increases by Salary Level Levels 2 and 3 CHF 1’300.- Level 4 CHF 1’400.- Levels 5 and 6 CHF 1’500.- Levels 7 to 9 CHF 2’000.- Levels 10 and above CHF 2’500.- Please note: These amounts may be adjusted depending on economic conditions (e.g., inflation). For individuals hired during the year, the increase is calculated on a pro rata basis. For any questions, it is recommended to contact the PEXP Partner of your unit. Doctoral Assistants, Postdocs, and Scientific Assistants Doctoral assistants, postdocs, and scientific assistants are part of EPFL staff, with the following salary scales. Postdocs 2026 Gross Annual Salary ( including the 13th salary month) 1 st year 2 nd year 3 rd year 4 th year 88,000 89,500 91,000 92,500 An increment of CHF 1,500 per year is granted, provided that budgetary resources and economic conditions allow (additional inflation adjustments may apply). For staff members hired during the year, the increase is calculated on a pro rata basis. An increment of CHF 1,500 per year is granted, provided that budgetary resources and economic conditions allow (additional inflation adjustments may apply). For staff members hired during the year, the increase is calculated on a pro rata basis.
